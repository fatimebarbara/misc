What happens

Everyone answers, then we reveal — and talk about why the obvious answer wasn't the right one.

The point: we can be fluent, confident, and still completely wrong. That goes for us, and for the machine.

Together · build it live

Build a great prompt, step by step

From a lazy prompt to a usable one.

15 MIN ↻
Two ladders, from our own work
  • Drafting an email to a beneficiary — the reply we all write again and again.
  • A presentation from a tender specification — a long, dense document into a usable draft.

One thing added at a time, re-run at every step, so you see what made the difference.

A good prompt fixes structure. Only checking fixes facts.
