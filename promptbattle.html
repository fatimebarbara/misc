Prompt battle:
explain HaDEA three ways.

One subject. Three very different listeners. Only one thing changes in your prompt — and everything changes in the answer.

Your brief

Write a prompt that explains what HaDEA does — to your audience. Same organisation, same facts. A completely different answer.

About 5 minutes to build it. Then we run them, and the room decides which one lands best.

This is the block that does the heavy lifting. Spend most of your time here.

3

Rules

Essential

The guardrails. What it must not do — and the limits it has to respect.

Given
  • No invented figures, budgets or dates.
  • Flag anything you are not sure about instead of guessing.
  • No jargon and no acronyms unless you explain them.
  • Nothing confidential goes into the prompt.
Your idea

What would ruin this for your audience? Ban it.

GivenPlain text we can read aloud in front of the room.

Role

Optional · last

Who the AI is speaking as. It's the block everyone reaches for first — and it changes the least. Add it only if the voice matters.

Your idea

Whose voice is telling this? (Skip it if it doesn't add anything.)

Your prompt

Fill the blocks above, then press "Build my prompt".

Before you present it

Two minutes that decide the round
  • Check the facts. A good prompt fixes structure and voice. It does not fix facts. Anything about what HaDEA does, or any number, gets checked against the real source before you read it out.
  • Bring the prompt, not just the answer. When you present, show what you wrote as well as what came back. That's the part everyone learns from.
  • If it's dull, look at block 2. Nine times out of ten the answer is flat because the audience wasn't described sharply enough.
