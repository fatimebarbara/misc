🔍 Objectives-Deliverables-Timeline Consistency Check

Handout for HaDEA Colleagues | Calls for Tenders & Calls for Grants

Objective

What This Check Does

This consistency check ensures that the objectives, tasks, deliverables, and timelines in a tender Specification, Terms of Reference (ToR), or grant Call Documentation are internally coherent, logically connected, properly sequenced, and achievable within the stated contract/grant duration and budget.

It identifies misalignments, missing dependencies, compressed schedules, and objective-deliverable gaps before the call is published, reducing contract/grant management risk and contractor/grantee disputes.

Applicable to: Service contracts, Supplies/Works tenders, Research studies, Grant programmes, Action grants, Scholarships, Infrastructure projects.

🔐 Secure Analysis Protocol

Copy ONLY Relevant REDACTED Sections | Use Internal Tools Strictly

STEP 1: IDENTIFY RELEVANT SECTIONS

From your Call documentation, copy ONLY the sections needed for consistency analysis:

  • Objectives & scope of work
  • Tasks and sub-tasks
  • Deliverables & acceptance criteria
  • Timeline & milestones
  • Contract duration & budget ceiling
  • Any work package or phase descriptions

Do NOT copy: Detailed budget breakdowns, evaluator names, strategic priorities, internal decision criteria.

STEP 2: APPLY MANDATORY REDACTION

Before pasting, remove or anonymize ALL sensitive information using the categories below.

STEP 3: USE INTERNAL TOOLS ONLY

STRICTLY: Only use Claude Desktop (installed locally) or an organizational AI tool explicitly authorized by your security/IT officer.

NEVER use: Public AI services (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude.ai), cloud collaboration platforms without restricted access, or any external service.

STEP 4: RUN ANALYSIS

Paste the redacted sections + this prompt into your internal tool. Receive 5 tables + prioritized issues.

✓ Redacted document + internal tool only = Safe, compliant, confidential analysis
