A2 Unit Away Day · Moderator Guide

Working with AI,
hands on.

Moderators: Fatime & Valentina Audience: A2 unit colleagues Setting: Away day · playful, hands-on
The aim of the session

To grow the A2 unit's knowledge and confident use of AI — during the away day, in a playful way. Less lecture, more experience: colleagues should leave curious, a little braver, and with at least one thing they can use on Monday.

Run of show

00
Opening

Opening the room

Lead: Fatime~10 min
A few minutes to open the room — curiosity over fear.
Purpose
  • Open the room and set a warm, human tone.
  • Turn any fear or scepticism into curiosity.
  • Frame the day: AI expands us, it doesn't replace us.
What it is

A short, human opening — creating curiosity and highlighting how tools don't shrink us, they expand us. It sets the tone for everything after: curiosity over fear, judgement over hype.

★
Warm-up · on our feet

Where do we stand?

Lead: Fatime & Valentina~8 min
Where do we stand? Stand where you feel.

Fatime introduces the session and leads the warm-up. At each round, Valentina asks a few people about the reasons for where they positioned themselves — a mini-interview, about a minute per person.

Purpose
  • Energise the group and break the ice.
  • Surface the room's diverse perspectives on AI adoption.
  • Create a shared starting point for the deeper conversations later.
Method — spectrum positioning

Participants physically place themselves along an imaginary line to show their stance. It gets bodies moving and makes the room's range visible to everyone.

01
Together · quiz

How wrong are we about AI?

Lead: Fatime & Valentina~10 min
Phones out — how wrong are we about AI?

Fatime prepares the questions on Slido, introduces the set-up, and reads the questions and answers. Valentina runs the mini-reflection series — a short feedback moment or mini-interview after each question, or after every two. It runs live on people's phones; we reveal and talk each answer through together.

Purpose
  • Meet our own assumptions about AI — and watch a few of them fall.
  • Set up the day's real lesson: we can be fluent, confident, and still wrong.
The set-up

Fatime opens the quiz from her own notes — the neuroplasticity framing and "we don't see reality, we see our interpretation of it."

How it runs
  1. People join the online quiz on their phones.
  2. One question at a time: everyone answers, then we reveal.
  3. After each reveal, a quick word on why the common answer is wrong — kept light, curious, never a lecture.
02
Hands-on · teams

Prompt battle

Lead: Valentina~15 min
Same task, two teams — the funniest prompt wins.

Fatime introduces the challenges. Valentina forms the groups (or we pre-form them before the event), moderates, and invites volunteers to present. The funniest result wins.

Purpose
  • Discover that the prompt — not the tool — does the work.
  • Loosen everyone up: play first, learn second.
Two challenges

Round 1 · the movie trailer. Each team prompts AI to write a dramatic 1–2 minute film-trailer script about the unit's work — "In a world where deadlines never sleep…".

Round 2 · explain HaDEA three ways. Same subject, three wildly different audiences — a five-year-old, an alien, a sceptical minister. The clearest possible proof that the prompt shapes everything.

How it runs
  1. Groups form, or are pre-assigned before the event. About five minutes per round to craft the best prompt.
  2. Teams that want to share present their result live; Valentina keeps it moving and warm.
  3. The room votes — the funniest one wins.
The learning to surface

When teams present, show the prompt next to the result. The funniest team almost always wrote the richest prompt. That bridges straight into the next section.

03
Together · build it live

Build a great prompt, step by step

Lead: Fatime~15 min
Same task — add a layer, watch it get better.

Fatime leads. We build one prompt together, live, on a real task from our world — a presentation and speech for an info day, addressed to national authorities.

Purpose
  • Show — surprisingly — how much better the result gets when the prompt is built with care.
  • Leave everyone with a repeatable recipe they can use on Monday.
The task

A short presentation and speech for an info day — the kind we give to national authorities about a newly published call. We start with a lazy prompt and add one layer at a time, re-running after each so the room sees the jump.

The reveal

Put version one and the final version side by side. The tool never changed — the prompt did. That gap is the point.

04
Together · your work

A recurring task, solved

Lead: Valentina?~40 min
The task you do again and again — solved.

We run a short survey beforehand — colleagues send in their recurring tasks — and build this section from what comes back.

Purpose

Make it personal — and immediate.

What it is

Based on the survey results, we pick a few recurring, time-eating tasks and work through them together — so people leave with something real, not hypothetical.

05
Framing · inspiration

Use cases — from outside work

Lead: Fatime~20 min
The breadth — health, apps, knowledge, keynotes, video.

Fatime leads — a quick, inspiring tour of what AI can do beyond the office.

Purpose

See the breadth — then find what fits your week. Use cases from outside work, to open people's imagination to what's possible.

What it is

A quick tour of the everyday ways AI helps. Based on the survey results, the content will be developed — a few to draw from:

  • Support on health and nutrition
  • Connected apps
  • Knowledge management
  • Keynote writing
  • Video generation
06
Together · close

Conclusion — what we take back

Lead: Valentina~10 min
From insight to intention — what will you take back?

Valentina runs the close — turning the day's energy into intention.

Purpose

Land the day: move from "that was interesting" to "here's what I'll do differently."

The questions
  • What change will you introduce in your practice?
  • What's the first thing you'll try this week?
  • What surprised you most today?
