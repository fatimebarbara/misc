Decisions requested

Four this edition, each tied to the indicator that produced it.

A

Fill the two vacant B.1 posts, or set CLOUDY a separate 130-day target for 2027

CLOUDY has been above the 100-day commitment for three consecutive cohorts while every other programme has come in under it. Unit B.1's establishment plan has been flat since 2023 and its vacancy rate is 12.9%. The Committee is asked to choose between the resource and the target, rather than restating the target.

From indicators 1 and 8 · Grants, StaffCapacity

B

Move the November–December payment cut-off forward by two weeks

November and December clear 69% of payments inside the limit against 94% in the rest of the year, in every year on record. A six-year stable pattern is a scheduling choice, not a capacity failure, and moving the internal deadline costs nothing.

From indicators 2 and 3 · Payments, BudgetExecution

C

Shift summer reporting deadlines to September at grant-preparation stage

Reports due in July and August arrive on time 34% and 38% of the time against roughly 76% in every other month. Either move those deadlines when the grant is prepared, or stop counting them in the corporate KPI. Continuing to report a blended figure that conceals both is the one option with nothing to recommend it.

From indicator 5 · Reporting

D

Extend the widening outreach pilot to ESPRESSO and NOVA before the 2027 calls

Widening coordinator share has risen from 19.2% to 24.2% and is now within a point of target — the pilot worked. It has not reached NOVA, which sits at 8.0%. Since widening and non-widening applicants succeed at almost the same rate, the remaining gap is in who applies, and outreach is the instrument that moves it.

From indicator 6 · Grants, Proposals, Beneficiaries

Opening

Good morning. Two things improved this period, two did not, and one programme is doing something the agency average is hiding.

First the good news, because there is some. Time to grant is at 94 days year to date — the first time the agency has come in under the 100-day commitment. And widening participation has gone from 19.2% to 24.2%, which puts us within a point of a target we have missed every year since 2019. The outreach pilot is the only thing in the data that changed, so I would extend it.

The programme the average is hiding is CLOUDY, at 127 days against everyone else's 81 to 97. It has been above target for three cohorts. Unit B.1 has been at a flat establishment plan since 2023 with a 12.9% vacancy rate, and nothing else in the workbook moves with it. Decision A asks you to choose between the posts and the target.

The one that got worse is reporting compliance: 66.4% on time, down from 73.3%, against a target of 90%. Two cuts explain nearly all of it — July and August deadlines, and first-time coordinators. Neither is a compliance problem, which is why Decision C is about the calendar rather than about enforcement.

Everything else is stable. Payments are at 97.5% inside the limit with interest down to eleven thousand euro, error rate is under materiality at 1.46%, and execution is at 98.7%. Four decisions, and I would like to start with A.
